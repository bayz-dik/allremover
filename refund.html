Refund Policy

Last updated: 16 August 2026

AllRemover Pro unlocks instantly after payment, so subscriptions are generally non-refundable. Please read the details below.

Digital service, instant access

AllRemover Pro is a digital subscription. The moment your payment is confirmed, all Pro features unlock immediately on your device. Because the service is delivered instantly and in full, payments are non-refundable once Pro has been activated.

Monthly billing

Pro is billed per month at Rp20,000. Each payment covers one month of access. We do not automatically charge you again; when a month ends, you choose whether to renew. This means you are never charged for a period you did not actively pay for.

Exceptions

We will consider a refund only in these cases:

  • Duplicate charge: you were accidentally charged more than once for the same period.
  • Payment taken but Pro not delivered: your payment succeeded but Pro features did not unlock, and we are unable to resolve it for you.

In these cases, contact us within 7 days of the charge with your payment details and we will investigate and refund if verified.

How to request

Email bayuandk4@gmail.com with your payment date, amount, and the email used. Approved refunds are returned via the original payment method and may take several business days depending on the payment provider.

Cancellation

To cancel, simply don't renew. Your Pro access remains active until the end of the month you paid for, then returns to the free tier. No fees.
