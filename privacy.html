Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 August 2026

Short version: your photos are processed entirely in your own browser and are never uploaded to any server.

Who we are

AllRemover is a free tool that removes the background from photos. It runs at www.temanbaik.web.id. This page explains what data is and isn't collected when you use it.

Your photos

AllRemover removes backgrounds using an AI model that runs on your own device, inside your browser. This means:

  • Your images are never uploaded to us or any server.
  • We never see, store, or have access to your photos.
  • Everything happens locally; your images stay on your device.

The only time the site uses the internet is a one-time download of the AI engine when you first use it, after which it works offline.

Data we collect

We do not run our own accounts, logins, or analytics database, so we do not collect personal information directly. A small setting (whether you've unlocked the demo "Pro" features) is stored locally in your browser and never leaves your device.

Advertising

To keep AllRemover free, the site may show ads from a third-party ad network (currently Adsterra). These networks may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads. This is standard for ad-supported sites and is separate from your photos, which the ad network never receives. If we later add Google AdSense, Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits; you can learn more and opt out at Google Ads Settings.

Third-party links

The site may include affiliate or donation links to other services (for example Saweria, or partner products). Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we're not responsible for their content or practices.

Children

AllRemover is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out via the donation/contact links on the main page.
